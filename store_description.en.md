# Ketchup Timer — Chrome Web Store Description (English)

### **Name**
Ketchup Timer

### **Short Description**
A unique Pomodoro timer featuring a ketchup bottle concept. Track your focus time with daily, weekly, and monthly visualizations.

### **Detailed Description**
Ketchup Timer is a Pomodoro timer browser extension built around a ketchup bottle visual metaphor. It displays your remaining session time directly on the browser toolbar icon and automatically records completed focus sessions as visual statistics.

[ 🚀 CORE FEATURES ]

- Dynamic Ketchup Bottle Icon: The toolbar icon updates continuously in real time. During Focus sessions, the bottle flips upside down and pours ketchup out as time elapses. During Short Break and Long Break sessions, the bottle stands upright and refills from the top. When paused, the stream stops while preserving the current bottle orientation and level.
- Focus Time Statistics and Period Navigation: Completed Focus sessions are automatically logged in minutes based on your local timezone. Review your focus trends in Daily (7 days), Weekly (4 weeks), and Monthly (6 months) bar charts, and browse past periods using the previous and next navigation buttons.
- Structured Pomodoro Cycle Management: Transitions cleanly across Focus, Break, and Long Break phases, tracking completed cycles automatically.
- One-Click System Notifications: Receives a system alert when each phase finishes. Click the action button on the notification to start the next phase immediately without reopening the popup.
- Built-in Multi-Language Support: Supports English, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese across the popup interface, charts, and notifications. Automatically detects your browser language and lets you switch languages at any time.

[ ✨ KEY HIGHLIGHTS ]

- Glanceable Status in the Toolbar: Distinguish Focus and Break phases immediately by bottle orientation, ketchup level, and pouring direction without interrupting your workflow.
- Minimal-Friction Workflow: Start the next session straight from the completion notification and review progress inside a compact popup interface.
- Lightweight Manifest V3 Architecture: Runs efficiently using standard browser extension APIs, automatically closing background animation helpers when the timer is paused or stopped.

[ 💡 HOW TO USE ]

1. Click the Ketchup Timer icon in your browser toolbar.
2. Click "Start" to begin a Focus session.
3. Monitor remaining time directly on the animated ketchup bottle icon while working, or use "Pause", "Resume", and "Reset" as needed.
4. When the phase completion notification appears, click "OK - Start Next Phase" to begin your break and refill the bottle.
5. Click "View Statistics" at the bottom of the popup to switch between Daily, Weekly, and Monthly bar charts and navigate historical periods.

[ 🛠 CUSTOMIZATION ]

Adjust the following settings directly in the popup:
- Focus duration: 1 to 60 minutes (default: 25 minutes)
- Break duration: 1 to 60 minutes (default: 5 minutes)
- Long Break duration: 1 to 60 minutes (default: 15 minutes)
- Cycles before Long Break: 1 to 10 cycles (default: 4 cycles)
- Display language: English, 日本語, 한국어, 简体中文, 繁體中文 (changeable even while the timer is running)
